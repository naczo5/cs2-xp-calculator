How XP is calculated

XP decides your profile rank, your annual service medal and whether you leave the week with a care package. CS2 pays it two ways, and only one of them cares how you play.

Rounds won, nothing else

In Premier, Competitive, Wingman and Rush, only the rounds your team wins count. Kills, assists, MVP stars and K/D add nothing. A 13–8 Premier win pays 390 base XP, or 1,560 XP at the 4× bonus. Lose 8–13 and you still keep 240 base XP.

This makes the round-based modes predictable: the scoreline is the whole payout. Rush and Wingman pay half as much per round as Premier, but their matches end much sooner, which is why Rush leads the rate table.

Scoreboard points, scaled

Casual, Deathmatch, Retakes and Arms Race pay per scoreboard point, with a different multiplier for each. A 500-point Deathmatch is 100 base XP; 35 points in Casual is 140; 30 points in Retakes is 60; 50 weapon score in Arms Race is 50.

Deathmatch is the most consistent solo option: a fixed 10-minute clock and constant respawns. A 750-point session is 150 base XP, or 600 XP at the 4× bonus.

The weekly bonus, drawn to scale

Every profile rank takes 5,000 XP. Your bonus shrinks as you earn XP after the Wednesday reset, then drops to a reduced rate once the pool is spent.

Your tier is set by XP already earned this week, not by the mode you pick.

Weekly care package

Your first 5,000 XP each week earns one profile rank and the care package. Pick 2 of 4 items: a weapon case, a weapon finish and two graffiti sprays. Extra levels in the same week do not give a second package.

Fastest route at full bonus: about 2 hours of Rush, or four Premier wins.

Service medals

Reaching rank 40 resets your level and awards the year's medal. Every 200,000 XP after that upgrades its color.

Questions players ask

Short answers to the parts that still trip people up.

How is XP calculated in Counter-Strike 2?

It depends on the mode family:

Rounds-won modes. Premier and Competitive pay rounds won × 30; Wingman and Rush pay rounds won × 15. Kills, assists, MVP stars and score add nothing here.

Score-based modes. Casual pays score × 4.0, Retakes score × 2.0, Arms Race score × 1.0 and Deathmatch score × 0.2.

The selected weekly bonus multiplies the result. At 4×, every base XP point is worth four.

What is the fastest way to earn XP?

Rush (3v3) has the highest average rate at about 43.7 XP/min, and short 4-round-lead wins can reach roughly 69 XP/min. For solo play, Deathmatch is the most consistent at about 40 XP/min. Premier pays the largest single match, up to 1,560 XP on a win at 4×, but takes about 32 minutes.

When does the weekly reset happen?

Every Wednesday at 01:00 UTC. In North America that is Tuesday evening: 6:00 PM PDT or 9:00 PM EDT, depending on daylight saving. In Europe it is early Wednesday: 2:00 AM CET or 3:00 AM CEST. In Asia it is Wednesday 9:00 AM CST / 10:00 AM JST.

The reset restores the bonus pool and makes you eligible for a new care package.

What is Reduced XP, and how do you clear it?

Reduced XP starts after you earn more than 11,166 XP in one reset week, and comes with the in-game XP Overload badge. Base XP is multiplied by 0.175, an 82.5% cut.

You cannot remove it manually. It clears on the next weekly reset at Wednesday 01:00 UTC.

How much XP is the weekly drop?

5,000 XP, exactly one profile rank. Your first level-up each week awards the care package; you choose 2 of 4 items. Further levels that week do not award another package.

Do bonus XP and XP Overload count toward service medals?

Yes. All XP counts 1:1 toward profile levels and the 200,000 XP service medal. Reduced XP also slows medal progress, so the cheapest route is to play inside the 4× and 2× windows and stop when the pool runs out.
